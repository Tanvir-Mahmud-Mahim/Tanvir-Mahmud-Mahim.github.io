## Where the money goes

Spending follows the same discipline as the code, and supporters are acknowledged in release notes:

<ul>
  <li><strong>Compute time</strong> for the finite-element, k·p and quantum-noise calculations behind each release and its test suite.</li>
  <li><strong>Open-access fees</strong>, so the papers the tools reproduce remain readable without a subscription.</li>
  <li><strong>Validation hardware</strong>, as the tools mature toward experimental cross-checks.</li>
</ul>

## Other ways to support

<ul>
  <li><strong>Use the tools and cite them.</strong> Each repository carries a <code>CITATION.cff</code> and a DOI; citations and issue reports are the strongest support an early research tool can receive.</li>
  <li><strong>Star, watch or contribute on <a href="https://github.com/TaN-MM-Org" rel="noopener">GitHub</a>.</strong> The contribution bar is the release bar: changes that touch physics arrive with a test, constants arrive with their source.</li>
  <li><strong>Institutional and grant partnerships.</strong> For collaboration or sponsored development, write to <a href="mailto:tanvir.mahim@bracu.ac.bd">tanvir.mahim@bracu.ac.bd</a>.</li>
</ul>
